# 💬 Memories from Family & Friends

## From me Lokesh her Grandson
"She was an amazing cook. She used to make this amazing chicken curry. It was always very hot, super tangy. She used to make sure I had the most amount of pieces"

"We loved playing rummy together. Mom thatha and I used to play rummy. We didn't use any cash; she didn't like that at all. But we didn't need it for all the fun we had. Her favorite catchphrase was joker...beeker, when she was looking for a joker in the set, if she didn't get one in her turn, she would say beeker.

"We played board games that she never understood, most monopoly but she would sit and play with mom and me. She wouldn't know what to do, but she would still play and do exactly as I asked her when playing the game"

"She and thatha bought me my first smartphone a Sony Ericsson. It was expensive back in the day, but she made sure I got it."

"She also got me my first bike, it was one with all the gears. She took me to the cycle shop and made sure I had it"

"She loved to sit in the balcony with thata. She was a bit short so she would let her feet hang from the seat and keep shaking it. We would have coffee or tea. We would always have some snacks. We would watch all the people walking by near our temple across the street"

"She loved wearing 2 bindhis. And always had a thiruneer in her forehead and kumkum at the top of her head"
